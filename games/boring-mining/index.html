LUNAR ARCADE PRESENTS

THE BORING MINING GAME

Mare Ingenii, 2091. You are DRONE-01 of colony BORE-1 — one unit among many, and the only one with a pilot. Bore the regolith, haul ore and ice home, lay beacons the rest of the swarm will follow, and print more of yourself. Somewhere across the mare, HELIOS EXTRACTION is doing exactly the same thing, and there is only one seam worth having.

An original fan-made game for the Lunar Arcade. Inspired by the 1991 colony-sim classic — no original code, art or data is used or distributed here.

OPERATIONS MANUAL

HOW TO PLAY

PILOTING DRONE-01

  • WASD or arrows — fly
  • SPACE (hold) — bore the rock you are facing
  • Q — drop an ORE BEACON: miners follow it
  • E — drop a RALLY BEACON: guards converge on it
  • 1 print miner   2 print guard   3 autofab
  • M beacon overlay   P pause   ESC quit
  • F — autoplay: hand DRONE-01 to the colony AI
  • [ ] — simulation speed, 0.25× to 8×

Cargo is picked up and unloaded automatically — bore a seam until it breaks, then fly home through your own tunnels.

Autoplay runs the whole colony for you — it prospects by scent, hauls, drops its own beacons, defends the fabricator and eventually mounts an assault. Touch any movement key and you take the controls straight back.

THE SWARM

  • Miners wander, smell ore through rock, bore toward it and haul it home — laying a scent trail on the return trip that other miners follow. That trail is how a productive tunnel becomes a highway.
  • Guards patrol the tunnels and run down any rally beacon or distress scent. They are how you take ground.
  • Beacons evaporate. A route only stays busy while it keeps paying.

THE FABRICATOR

  • ORE — structure. Printing bodies.
  • ICE — volatiles. Coolant and life support.
  • POWER — solar. Charges fast in the lunar day, barely at all in the night. Print while the sun is up.

THE OPPOSITION

  • Helios drones mine the same seams and raid your hub. Bore into their fabricator to end the contract.
  • Tube crawlers live in the deep lava tubes and eat anything with a heat signature. Neither colony owns them.
  • If DRONE-01 is destroyed, your link jumps to the nearest surviving unit. You only lose when the colony does.
