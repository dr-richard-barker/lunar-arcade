FROM THE COLONY SIM

The Boring Mining Game

A lunar ISRU mining programme run one drone at a time. You pilot DRONE-01; the rest of the swarm is autonomous. Bore tunnels through regolith and basalt, haul ilmenite and volatiles back to the plant, and drop scent beacons the swarm will follow — a productive seam becomes a highway because your drones keep reinforcing the trail home. Every tonne delivered builds the surface plant out another module, and the surplus flies to Earth orbit on a cargo stack. Meanwhile Helios Extraction works the same rock from the far side of the mare, and only one fabricator walks away from this contract.

  • Diffusing, evaporating scent fields drive the whole swarm — no scripted paths
  • Ore smell propagates through rock, so drones bore toward what they can smell
  • A surface plant that grows: solar arrays, high-gain dish, volatile tanks, radiators, habitat dome — then launches cargo to Earth
  • Solar economy on a compressed lunar day/night cycle
  • Lose your drone and the link jumps to the nearest unit — you only die when the swarm does
  • Autoplay hands the controls to an agent that wins unaided, plus a 0.25×–8× speed dial

FROM THE FARM SIM

Lunar Farm

Pressurised grow halls on the rim of a lava-tube skylight, dragged out to whatever size you want. Balance light hours against power, water against recycling losses, and crop choice against a colony that has to be fed — through a night that lasts a fortnight and takes the solar arrays with it. Regolith is not soil until you make it soil: fresh beds are sterile dust and only come good as you work roots and stubble back through them.

  • Drag out halls, roads and rail; freight trains then work the line on their own
  • A closed carbon loop — a surplus farm exports carbon inside the food it sells
  • Crops that have actually flown: Veggie lettuce, VEG-05 tomato, PH-04 chile, Apogee wheat
  • Telemetry you can read as a report to Earth or as a note to the settlers
  • Auto-manage, a sandbox, and a league of past runs with JSON export

FROM THE TOWER SIM

Lunar Habitat

The vertical-builder formula, upended: the surface is the worst floor in the building. Tower upward for sunlight, observatories and comms — or excavate downward, level by level, where the rock itself is your radiation shielding. Run shafts, keep commutes short, bank two weeks of power before every lunar night, and grow a six-person outpost into a chartered lunar capital.

  • Dig down 24 levels, build up 26 — excavation gets pricier with depth, safety gets better
  • 28-day lunar cycle: 14 days of solar bounty, 14 days of battery-draining darkness
  • Transit-distance scoring: every module is rated on its commute back to an airlock
  • Solar flares, moonquakes, micrometeorites — and regolith shield caps to survive them
  • Six charter tiers, from Outpost to Lunar Capital, each unlocking new modules
  • Autopilot mode — a colony director AI that builds, digs and balances the books itself
  • A Mission Control dashboard reporting growth curves and a board grade back to Earth
  • Sandbox mode: every module free, every tier unlocked
  • A persistent league table that scores every finished run on a dozen itemised factors

ABOUT THESE GAMES

These are original games, written from scratch in vanilla JavaScript. They are tributes to a genre, not ports, remasters or reskins: no code, artwork, audio or data files from any commercial game are used, included or distributed here. Game mechanics — colony simulation, pheromone-trail pathing, resource economies — are ideas, and ideas are free to build on. The specific expression of the originals belongs to the people who made them, and stays with them.

If you own a copy of one of the classics that inspired these, play it. They hold up.
